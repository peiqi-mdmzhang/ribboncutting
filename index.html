The Problem Statement

Jess needs 200 pieces of ribbons, each of length 110 cm, to decorate a room. Ribbon is sold in rolls of 25 m (2500 cm) each.

What is the least number of rolls of ribbon that Jess needs to buy?

CRITICAL LESSON: Why Total-Length Division Fails!

🚫 Why dividing total length (200 × 110 cm ÷ 2500 cm) is WRONG:

Total length division assumes you can join or glue the 80 cm offcuts from each roll together. In reality, offcut ribbons cannot be glued and are wasted!

❌ Why 9 rolls is WRONG:

9 rolls only give 9 × 22 = 198 pieces. Jess would be 2 ribbons short! Therefore, Jess needs 10 rolls.

✅ Correct Method:

200 ÷ 22 = 9 remainder 2  ➔  9 + 1 = 10 rolls

Step 4

Finding Total Rolls Needed

Complete Step-by-Step Solution Key

1. Convert units to cm 25 m = 2500 cm
2. Usable pieces per roll 2500 ÷ 110 = 22 remainder 80 cm ➔ 22 full pieces per roll
3. Divide required pieces by 22 200 ÷ 22 = 9 remainder 2 ➔ 9 rolls give 198 pieces (need 2 more)
4. Round up for the remainder 9 + 1 = 10 rolls Least number of rolls needed = 10
